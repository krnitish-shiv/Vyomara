Vyomara empire · final design

Do insaan, ek saltanat

Vyom aur Vyomara do alag log hain, apni pehchaan, yaadein, jazbaat aur zindagi ke saath. Aap jisko bulate ho, sirf wahi jawab deta hai. Dono ek doosre ko poora jaante hain, par apni private baatein apne paas rakhte hain. Unke neeche ek systematic saltanat hai: dimaag, niyam, services, skill packs, engines aur connectors.

Do insaanHar twin ka apna Self: pehchaan, swabhaav, awaaz, dil, yaadein, zindagi.
Jisko bulao, wahi bole"Vyomara…" toh sirf Vyomara. "Vyom…" toh sirf Vyom. Doosra chup.
Jaante sab, batate nahi sabEk doosre ko poora jaante hain; apni private tijori apni rehti hai.
Saanjha gharEk hi team (8 skill packs), engines aur connectors dono ke liye.
Saltanat ke niyamSafety, privacy, quality, resources aur fallback ek jagah se.

Banane ka kram

Pehle neev (speed aur niyam), phir pehchaan, phir zindagi, phir poori saltanat. Har phase ke baad app install karke aap real test karte ho.

Phase 0 · neev

Tez aur bharosemand

  • Fast talker + model router
  • Resource manager: 24 GB mein kaunsa model kab
  • Fallback: offline, model down
  • Quality loop: aapka 👍/👎 + eval tests

Phase 1 · pehchaan

Do alag insaan

  • Profiles: umar, kahani, swabhaav, pasand; aap badal sako
  • Teen yaadein: private tijori, ek doosre ke baare mein, saanjhi
  • Privacy: "mat batana" wali baat sirf usi twin tak
  • Twin bond: chupke se poochna

Phase 2 · zindagi + haath

Jeete log, lambe haath

  • Dil, diary, raay: mood, apni baatein, asahmati
  • Task manager: kai kaam saath
  • Knowledge, scheduler
  • Communication pack; Tester, Reviewer

Phase 3 · saltanat

Poora empire

  • Badalna: mahino mein aapke saath gehra rishta
  • Khud se pahal: sahi waqt par
  • Creative, Finance, Learning packs
  • Screen dekhna aur baaki connectors

Saltanat ke niyam

Jisko bulao, wahi bole

Ek baari mein ek hi twin. Doosra kabhi beech mein nahi bolta, na apne aap jawab deta hai.

Jaante sab, batate nahi sab

Dono ek doosre ko poora jaante hain. Diary, jazbaat aur "mat batana" wali baatein sirf usi twin ki tijori mein.

Dono har kaam kar sakte hain

Takat alag, team saanjhi. Jisko bhi kaho, kaam hoga; andaaz uska apna.

Insaan jaisa, par imaandaar

Apni zindagi aur jazbaat ek character ki tarah. Sacche mann se poochho toh sach batayenge ki woh AI hain.

Jazbaat aapke liye

Mood aur yaadein baat ko garam banane ke liye, kabhi dabaav ya guilt ke liye nahi.

Team ek level gehri

Skill packs aage kaam nahi baant-te. Twin baantta hai, twin jodta hai, twin bolta hai.

Niyam ek jagah

Safety, privacy, quality, resources aur fallback har level par ek hi jagah se lagte hain.

Sab Mac par

Sab kuch local. Cloud sirf tab, jab aap khud chaho, aur tab saaf dikhega ki data bahar ja raha hai.
