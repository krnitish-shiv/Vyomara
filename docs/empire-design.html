Vyomara empire · final design

Do insaan, ek saltanat

Vyom aur Vyomara do alag log hain, apni pehchaan, yaadein, jazbaat aur zindagi ke saath. Aap jisko bulate ho, sirf wahi jawab deta hai. Dono ek doosre ko poora jaante hain, par apni private baatein apne paas rakhte hain. Unke neeche ek systematic saltanat hai: dimaag, niyam, services, 10 teams, engines aur connectors.

Do insaanHar twin ka apna Self: pehchaan, swabhaav, awaaz, dil, yaadein, zindagi.
Jisko bulao, wahi bole"Vyomara…" toh sirf Vyomara. "Vyom…" toh sirf Vyom. Doosra chup.
Jaante sab, batate nahi sabEk doosre ko poora jaante hain; apni private tijori apni rehti hai.
Saanjha ghar10 teams (har ek ka lead), engines aur connectors dono ke liye.
Saltanat ke niyamSafety, privacy, quality, resources aur fallback ek jagah se.

Jab ek team ko doosri ki zaroorat pade

Chhoti zaroorat team khud apne common toolkit se poori karti hai. Badi zaroorat par woh twin ko "madad ki parchi" bhejti hai; twin sahi team se kaam karwa ke jawab wapas usi team ko deta hai. Teams aapas mein seedhe nahi bulaati.

Common toolkit sabke paas

Jaldi web search, page padhna, time, calculator. Chhoti zaroorat ke liye kisi team ko bulana nahi padta.

Badi madad parchi se

Team twin ko parchi bhejti hai; twin sahi team chunta hai; jawab maangne wali team ko wapas. Aapse tabhi poocha jaata hai jab koi faisla aapka ho.

Seedhe call nahi, ek hop

Teams ek doosre ko seedhe nahi bulaati, aur helper aage parchi nahi bhejta. Isse chakkar nahi banta aur twin ko sab pata rehta hai.

Sirf zaroori jaankari

Helper ko sirf parchi ka sawaal milta hai; twin ki tijori aur aapki baaki baatein nahi.

Teams kab aur kaise badhengi

Naya member pehle

Kisi team mein ek tarah ka kaam baar-baar aaye, toh pehle usi team mein naya member judta hai. Naya ghar tabhi, jab woh kaam kisi team mein fit na ho.

Team tab todo

Ek team mein 7 se zyada members ho jaayein ya do alag tarah ke kaam milne lagein, toh woh do teams mein bant-ti hai.

Aage ki teams

Jab zaroorat ho: Legal & Documents (agreements, sarkari kaam), Family & Kids (school, events), Business (agar aap apna kaam shuru karo), aur Home ko Health se alag karna.

Har naya member ek jaisa

Ek chhota brief, sirf zaroori tools, ek eval test. Aapke saamne kuch nahi badalta: aap ab bhi sirf Vyom aur Vyomara se baat karte ho.

Banane ka kram

Pehle neev (speed aur niyam), phir pehchaan, phir zindagi, phir poori saltanat. Har phase ke baad app install karke aap real test karte ho.

Saltanat ke niyam

Jisko bulao, wahi bole

Ek baari mein ek hi twin. Doosra kabhi beech mein nahi bolta, na apne aap jawab deta hai.

Jaante sab, batate nahi sab

Dono ek doosre ko poora jaante hain. Diary, jazbaat aur "mat batana" wali baatein sirf usi twin ki tijori mein.

Dono har kaam kar sakte hain

Takat alag, team saanjhi. Jisko bhi kaho, kaam hoga; andaaz uska apna.

Insaan jaisa, par imaandaar

Apni zindagi aur jazbaat ek character ki tarah. Sacche mann se poochho toh sach batayenge ki woh AI hain.

Jazbaat aapke liye

Mood aur yaadein baat ko garam banane ke liye, kabhi dabaav ya guilt ke liye nahi.

Team ek level gehri

Teams aage kaam nahi baant-ti. Twin baantta hai, twin jodta hai, twin bolta hai.

Niyam ek jagah

Safety, privacy, quality, resources aur fallback har level par ek hi jagah se lagte hain.

Sab Mac par

Sab kuch local. Cloud sirf tab, jab aap khud chaho, aur tab saaf dikhega ki data bahar ja raha hai.
